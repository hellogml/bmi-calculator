What Is the BMI Calculator?

The BMI Calculator is a free online tool that computes your Body Mass Index (BMI) — a widely used screening measure that uses your height and weight to estimate whether your weight falls within a healthy range. Developed in the 19th century by the Belgian mathematician Adolphe Quetelet and adopted by the World Health Organization as a standard health indicator, BMI provides a quick, accessible way to assess weight status for most adults.

This calculator supports both the metric system (centimeters and kilograms) and the imperial system (inches and pounds), making it convenient for users worldwide. After entering your measurements, the tool instantly returns your BMI value, places you in the appropriate WHO category (underweight, normal, overweight, or obese), and shows the healthy weight range for your specific height.

How Does the BMI Calculator Work?

The tool applies the standard BMI formula recognized by the World Health Organization. The calculation differs slightly depending on your unit system:

Metric Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Since you enter height in centimeters, the tool first divides by 100 to convert to meters, then squares the result and divides your weight by it.

Imperial Formula

BMI = [weight (lb) × 703] ÷ height (in)²

The factor 703 converts the imperial result so it matches the metric BMI scale, allowing the same category thresholds to apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a BMI Calculator?

A BMI Calculator is a free online tool that calculates your Body Mass Index — a standard measure that uses your height and weight to estimate whether your weight is in a healthy range. It works with both metric (cm, kg) and imperial (inches, pounds) units.

How do I use the BMI Calculator?

What is a healthy BMI range?

According to the World Health Organization, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal and healthy. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25.0 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30.0 or above is classified as obese.

Does BMI apply to everyone?

BMI is a general screening tool for adults and may not be accurate for athletes with high muscle mass, pregnant women, or the elderly. It does not measure body fat directly. For a complete health assessment, consult a healthcare professional.
