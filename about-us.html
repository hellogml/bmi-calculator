About Us

Welcome to BMI Calculator — a simple, fast, and privacy-first online tool built to help you check your Body Mass Index in an instant.

Our Mission

We believe health tools should be three things: fast, accurate, and private. Too many health calculators are slow, cluttered with pop-ups, or quietly collect your data. We set out to build the opposite — a clean utility that loads instantly, gives you a precise BMI the moment you click, and never sends your personal information to a server.

How Our Tool Works

When you enter your height and weight, the JavaScript on the page does all the math locally — applying the standard WHO BMI formula, classifying your result, and calculating your healthy weight range. There is no backend server processing your input, which means your measurements never leave your device.

What Makes Us Different

  • Privacy by design. No accounts, no data uploads, no tracking of your inputs.
  • Dual units. Switch between metric and imperial with one click.
  • WHO standard. Classification based on the World Health Organization's official categories.
  • Free for everyone. No paywalls, no sign-ups, no limits.

Who We Serve

Our tool is used by individuals tracking their wellness, fitness enthusiasts setting baselines, healthcare professionals doing quick screenings, and anyone curious about their BMI. Whether you are monitoring your health or just want a quick check, we are here to give you the answer in seconds.
